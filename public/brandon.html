The main idea

Road to one stall

A follow up in the same spirit as the series that made you. Same format, new sport, and this time it would be yours from the start.

A stall is when you catch the sack and hold it still on your foot. It looks simple and it really is not. It is the trick most people chase first, and it usually takes a while to land, which is what makes it worth filming.

Each episode could be fifteen to twenty five seconds. One attempt, one joke, ending on a running tally. Cheap to shoot, works anywhere, and every episode gives people a reason to look for the next one.

You mentioned wanting something ongoing rather than a single post. A running series felt like the most honest way to actually build that, rather than just promising it.

The fall campaign

Sack to school

A back to school clip tournament on Instagram. Schools send in their best hacky sack clips, your followers vote in story polls, and the school that wins gets custom sacks in their own school colors, with their logo on the center patch.

How it would work

  1. 1 You post an announcement explaining the tournament and what schools are playing for.
  2. 2 Schools send in clips by tagging you and us. We sort and moderate everything that comes in.
  3. 3 We build a bracket from the best entries. You reveal it however you like.
  4. 4 Your followers vote round by round in story polls, which is the part Instagram does best.
  5. 5 The winning school gets custom sacks in their own school colors, with their logo on the center patch.

Rough timing

  1. Sept 1 Announcement, so schools know what they are playing for.
  2. September Submissions open and start coming in.
  3. October Bracket reveal, then story polls begin round by round.
  4. Late Oct Semis and final, when schools tend to campaign hardest.
  5. November Winner announced, sacks made and delivered.

These dates are a suggestion, not a schedule. We can move any of it to fit what you already have planned.

What we would ask of you Showing up on camera. The announcement, the bracket reveal, reactions, the winner and the delivery.
What we would handle Everything behind it. Bracket graphics, poll copy, school outreach, moderation, design, production, shipping, your code and monthly reporting.

If the retirement bit is not for you

Then we try something else

The retirement angle is just one idea, and it is not the partnership. If it does not sit right, or you are still chasing basketball and it would send the wrong signal, we will happily drop it. Here are three other directions we would be just as glad to build.

Option A

The commissioner

No retiring at all. You are simply the commissioner of Sack to School, running the tournament with total authority and no explanation of where it came from.

Option B

Offseason training

Hacky sack as genuine footwork and coordination work, played completely straight. Keeps basketball fully in the picture.

Option C

Just the tournament

No series and no bit. You host Sack to School and nothing else. The smallest version, and we would still be glad to do it.
